Rules & Strategy

01

Objective

The goal is to capture the maximum territory (grid cells) on the board before the grid is completely filled or both players run out of valid moves.

02

Roll & Dimensions

On your turn, roll the two dice. The values rolled represent the length and width of the rectangle you must draw on the grid (e.g. rolling 3 and 5 means a 3x5 or 5x3 rectangle).

03

First Placement

Your very first placement must start exactly at your designated corner. Player 1 starts in the **top-left** corner. Player 2 starts in the **bottom-right** corner.

04

Adjacency Rules (Strict Edges)

Every subsequent rectangle must touch at least one cell on the flat side/edge (not diagonal corners) of your already captured rectangles. It cannot overlap with any placed cells.

05

Controls

Use the Rotate button (or press the Spacebar) to toggle the orientation of your rectangle before placing. Hover over the grid to preview the block and click to secure it.

06

Auto-Pass & Victory

If there is absolutely zero space on the board to fit your rolled dimensions under the strict rules, the match engine will automatically detect it, alert you, and pass your turn. The match ends when both players are forced to pass consecutively. The player with the largest captured area wins!
